Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026

VertexFX collects the information you provide directly: your name, email address, and account activity within this platform (positions, orders, transactions).

This information is used solely to operate your account — authentication, displaying your trading history, and account-related communication such as email verification.

Your password is never stored in plain text. Two-factor authentication secrets are stored securely and are never displayed after initial setup.

This platform does not sell or share your information with third parties. Data is not used for advertising.

You may request account deletion at any time through the Support page.
